# Catalogue rights and data quality

## Included information

The school supplied the catalogue and authorized public release of its book records. This release contains 1,026 records with title, author credits, publisher, year, ISBN, subjects, call number and historical copy count. IDs are catalogue identifiers, not borrower identifiers. The `cover` field is retained as an empty compatibility field.

These are factual bibliographic/inventory records, not copies of the books. No long-form descriptions, excerpts, jacket text, cover artwork, borrower data, staff records or authentication information are included. Author and publisher names identify the credited works; they do not imply endorsement of this project.

## Rights boundary

The MIT license covers first-party code/documentation and any rights the publisher holds in the catalogue's original selection or arrangement. It does not transfer copyright in the books, cover designs, third-party prose, publisher artwork, trademarks or other material belonging to others. Factual bibliographic metadata is distinct from those protected works.

The source catalogue does not record per-item metadata-provider licence evidence. This review does not certify an upstream chain of title or jurisdiction-specific database rights. Any future imported descriptions, images or externally licensed database material require their own permission and notices. Nothing in this release grants permission to redistribute full books.

All 1,024 original JPG covers were omitted because per-cover redistribution rights were not established. The source application already supported coloured title placeholders; this edition uses them for every entry and ignores cover URLs entirely. It does not fetch replacements from remote image services.

## Data quality

This is the school's 25 September 2026 snapshot, not a verified bibliographic authority or live circulation feed. Titles, authors, spelling, capitalization, subjects, call numbers and copy counts are preserved rather than silently corrected.

Known anomalies include 15 ISBN-field values with nonstandard ISBN lengths (one may be an ISSN), record 967's year `2205`, and record 119 containing an ISBN in its publisher field. These are documented data-quality issues, not evidence of borrower information. Verify identifiers and publication facts before relying on them for purchasing, cataloguing or research.

The 1,095 total recorded copies do not show how many books are currently available to borrow. No availability, loan or borrower lookup is provided.
